Privacy Policy

Sunnati. Last updated Rabi al-Awwal 1448 AH.

In short

Sunnati collects nothing about you. It asks for no account, no email and no phone number. Everything you record stays on your device, and the people who wrote the app have no way to see any of it.

Nothing leaves your device except in one place, which you enter knowingly: the nearby mosques screen, which by its nature must ask what is around you. That is set out under Location below.

What is kept on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage to keep your settings and what you have recorded, including:

  • the city or coordinates you chose, the calculation method and the Asr school
  • which adhkar, sunan and daily portion you have marked
  • how you prayed your five prayers, and your daily record
  • the verses you marked and any notes on them, where you stopped in the Mus'haf, and your khatma plan
  • your choices of appearance, text size and reciter

None of this leaves your device. You can export it as a single file or delete all of it from the settings screen at any time, and clearing the site's browser data erases it too.

Location

If you allow the app to know your location, it uses it to compute prayer times and the qibla direction on your device, and sends it to no server. You may instead pick a city from a list, in which case location is never requested.

The one exception is the nearby mosques screen. It sends your location to OpenStreetMap and its Overpass service to find which mosques are near you, and map tiles are requested from their server, which likewise carries the place you are looking at. None of this happens unless you open that screen yourself, and your name and device identifier are never sent with it.

What needs the internet

The app works offline after its first opening. The internet is needed only for recitation audio, map tiles on the mosques screen, and updates to the app itself.

Tapping «rate the app» opens its page in your phone's store, play.google.com or apps.apple.com, with nothing about you sent along. Tapping «send feedback» opens your mail app with a message addressed to us that carries the app's version, and it leaves your phone only if you send it yourself.

What the app does not have

  • no advertising and no ad networks
  • no tracking, no analytics and no device identifiers
  • no cookies for tracking purposes
  • no sharing or selling of any data, since there is no data to share

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects data from no one, and so collects none from children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will appear on this page and in the repository's release history.
