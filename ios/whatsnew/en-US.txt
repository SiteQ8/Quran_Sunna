Three new widgets: the dua of the day from the Quran and the Sunnah with its source, your tasbih with its count and target, and your prayers today with the ones you have prayed. The verse of the day has a button that turns to a verse from another surah, today's prayers show the Gregorian date under the Hijri one, and the mushaf now opens from a widget at its proper size.

Every widget wears the app's own typeface and an Islamic strapwork corner, in more sizes. Adhan and night prayer notifications arrive in the app's language, Settings has a switch to turn the adhan off or on, and the reminder to send blessings upon the Prophet arrives on time.

An independent review led to corrections: the Friday prayer is called Jumu'ah everywhere, weak narrations were removed from the virtues of the surahs, the spelling of the supplications was corrected, the iqama countdown runs to its end, and prayer times are right on the day the clocks change.
